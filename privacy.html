Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Summary

Codeberry does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app has no sign-up or login, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.

Information stored on your device

Your completed lessons, saved lessons and settings (such as text size and theme) are stored only on your device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

Tracking, analytics and advertising

Codeberry does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not share data with third parties.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Information from Apple

If you download Codeberry from the App Store, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated statistics, such as the number of downloads. This information cannot be used to identify you.

Children's privacy

Because Codeberry collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Any change to how the app handles data will also appear in a new version of the app.
