Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Codeberry has no sign-up or login. You can start reading straight away.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All lessons are inside the app, so you don't need an internet connection.

Where is my progress saved?

Your completed lessons and saved lessons are stored on your device only. If you delete the app or switch phones, your progress does not move with it.

How do I save a lesson for later?

Open a lesson and tap the bookmark icon at the top right. Find your saved lessons in the Saved tab.

How do I change the text size or switch to Dark mode?

Open the Settings tab. You can choose the text size and the theme (System, Light or Dark).

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings and tap “Reset progress”. You will be asked to confirm.

The app shows no lessons or looks empty. What should I do?

Close the app completely and open it again. If the problem continues, delete the app, install it again and contact us.

I found a mistake in a lesson.

We'd like to know. Email us the lesson title and what looks wrong.

Which languages are covered?

Swift, Kotlin and C++, with ten beginner lessons each.
